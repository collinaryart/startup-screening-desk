# Orvelle Diagnostics: analytical validation summary (September 2026)

## What was tested

The Orvelle rapid susceptibility test was run on 310 archived bacterial isolates from bloodstream infections, against the reference broth microdilution method.

## Results

- Categorical agreement with the reference method was 94.2% across 310 isolates.
- The median time to result was 92 minutes.
- Very major errors were 1.3%.

## Limits of this work

- These were archived isolates, not fresh patient samples. Clinical performance is not yet shown.
- Six antibiotics were covered. The commercial panel will need at least twelve.

## Next step

A 200-patient clinical study with a Singapore hospital laboratory, due to start in the first quarter of 2027.
